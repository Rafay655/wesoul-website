01 / Healthcare operations

Connecting the work around medical equipment.

The problem
Equipment records, service activity and maintenance documentation can become scattered across separate tools.
What we built
Calliper — a specialized biomedical service operations platform.
Intended outcome
Designed to bring equipment and service activity into one operational view. No measured client outcome is claimed.
Explore the product story
02 / Retail operations

Bringing the store into one working picture.

The problem
Sales, stock, customer balances and payment records are difficult to reconcile when managed independently.
What we built
SpanPOS — a connected retail operations platform.
Intended outcome
Designed to help teams consider sales, stock and payments together. Customer-specific results have not been supplied.
Explore the product story
03 / Procurement

Following a request all the way to receipt.

The problem
Procurement handoffs become fragmented across requests, approvals, vendors, quotations and purchasing records.
What we built
Reqzly — a procurement lifecycle platform.
Intended outcome
Designed to give procurement work a connected sequence and record. This is a product walkthrough, not a verified client case study.
