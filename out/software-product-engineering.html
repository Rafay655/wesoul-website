02 / When Custom Software Makes Sense

When should a business build custom software?

Custom software makes sense when the workflow, product opportunity or operating model cannot be supported effectively by standard software without excessive compromise.

Existing software cannot support the workflow.

Multiple systems create duplicated work.

A new digital product is being created.

Existing software has become restrictive.

Business differentiation depends on the process.
