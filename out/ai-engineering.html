03 / What Is an AI Agent?

What Is an AI Agent?

An AI agent is a software system that can interpret a request, reason over available information, use permitted tools or applications, and perform defined actions toward an outcome. In business environments, agents should operate within clear permissions, controls and approval boundaries.
