A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is Reqzly?+

Reqzly is a procurement lifecycle platform covering internal requisitions, vendor onboarding, quotation evaluation, purchasing and goods receipt management.

Where does the workflow start and end?+

The specified workflow starts with a requisition and moves through approval, vendor onboarding, quotation evaluation, purchase and goods receipt to a procurement record.

Can our approval and role requirements be reviewed?+

Yes. Request a demo and share your approval paths, user roles and audit requirements so WESOUL can confirm the current product fit.
